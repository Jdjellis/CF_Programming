Paces key off the athlete's 5k row PR. Percentages are perceived effort.
S1 is the main session (the priority), S2 is primarily aerobic, S3 is intervals/pacing.
Meso 1 (W1–4): testing, pacing. Meso 2: building volume and intensity. Meso 3: higher intensity, taper, retest.

## Sessions

### W0 S1
To begin: 5k Time Trial

Record your Time, average pace 500m, split, av wattage
Nice to also have Heart Rate data where possible.

### W1 S1
5 x 300m, rest 1 min between. 70%, 75%, 80%, 85%, 90% efforts respectively.
+
4 Sets;
Row 4 min @ 5k PR Pace + 2 sec, rest 60 seconds between.
*+2 seconds means +2 seconds slower than your set pace.

### W1 S2
Row 30 min @ Z1 building to Z3 through the 30 min.

### W1 S3
12 sets of:
Row 30 sec @ 85%
Row 30 sec @ 50%

Then, rest 5 min, into

10 sets of:
Row 30 sec @ 85%
Row 30 sec @ 50%

%s are percieved effort.
